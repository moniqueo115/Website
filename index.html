About Monique

Independent. Practical. Operations-first.

Monique Owens is an independent Property Operations Advisor with more than a decade of experience in construction, property operations, project management, and strategic leadership. As a fourth-generation general contractor, she brings a practical, operations-first perspective to real estate and portfolio management. Her work focuses on helping property owners and investment groups strengthen the systems behind growing portfolios, including reporting and visibility, vendor oversight, internal controls, operational accountability, and organizational structure. Monique specializes in identifying operational gaps that can create inefficiency, increase risk, or limit scalability—and developing practical frameworks to address them. Based in Houston, Texas, she works independently with owners and real estate organizations seeking greater clarity, control, and consistency across their operations.
